07 — Programs

Care,
in detail.

Open a program to go deeper on assessment, intervention, and ongoing care.

01Executive Health Assessment$12,000

Our ultimate executive physical expands or contracts around your age, health status, and priorities while minimizing the time required to complete it.

  • Pre-assessment interview and customized testing plan
  • Advanced labs and optional microbiome, hormone, and cancer screening panels
  • Whole-body MRI, cardiac CT angiography, echocardiography, and DEXA access
  • VO₂ max and body-composition assessment
  • Comprehensive physician examination and results review
  • Personalized action plan with longitudinal prompts
02Men’s & Women’s Hormone OptimizationContact for pricing

Physician-guided hormone evaluation for men and women, tailored to symptoms, goals, health history, and laboratory findings.

  • Comprehensive hormone and metabolic testing
  • Individualized treatment planning based on clinical need
  • Ongoing laboratory monitoring and symptom review
  • Nutrition, sleep, strength, and body-composition guidance
  • Longitudinal adjustments as needs and goals change
03Longevity, Epigenetics & Rejuvenation$6,000 / year

Epsilon combines evidence-based interventions with emerging research to measure biological age, identify modifiable risk, and build a targeted healthspan plan.

  • Epigenetic-age and biomarker testing options
  • Cardiac, cancer-risk, VO₂ max, and functional assessment
  • Individualized nutrition, exercise, and recovery recommendations
  • Physician-guided review of senolytics, metabolic therapies, and supplements when appropriate
  • Longitudinal progress mapping and proactive guidance
04Healing & RecoveryContact for pricing

A coordinated recovery plan for injury, surgery, hard training, and occupational fatigue. Programs may combine diagnostics, rehabilitation guidance, and advanced recovery modalities based on physician assessment.

  • Red light and hyperbaric oxygen therapy
  • Biologics, including PRP and other options when appropriate
  • IV support and recovery-focused protocols
  • Progress assessment and return-to-performance planning
05Microbiome & Gut HealthContact for pricing

Comprehensive assessment of the gut ecosystem and its relationship to metabolism, inflammation, immune function, and overall health.

  • Advanced stool and microbiome testing options
  • Dietary, prebiotic, probiotic, and lifestyle recommendations
  • Physician interpretation in the context of symptoms and health goals
  • Follow-up testing and longitudinal adjustment where appropriate
06Performance OptimizationContact for pricing

Measure the systems that drive physical and cognitive performance, then translate the data into practical interventions.

  • VO₂ max, DEXA body composition, movement, and recovery assessment
  • Sleep, nutrition, hormone, and metabolic review
  • Individualized training and recovery recommendations
  • Longitudinal tracking against performance goals
07Joint PreservationContact for pricing

A physician-led strategy to protect mobility, manage pain, and preserve joint function with diagnostics, rehabilitation, and advanced treatment options.

  • Clinical evaluation and imaging coordination
  • Strength, movement, and body-composition planning
  • PRP, PRF, stem-cell, exosome, and other biologic options when appropriate
  • Recovery modalities and ongoing progress review
08Weight & Metabolic HealthPrograms vary

Individualized metabolic care focused on sustainable weight loss, muscle preservation, body composition, and long-term health—not pounds alone.

  • Medication and non-medication pathways based on clinical need
  • GLP-1/GIP and other pharmacotherapy options when appropriate
  • DEXA measurement of fat mass, lean mass, visceral fat, and bone density
  • Nutrition, exercise, and maintenance planning
09Cancer Prevention & Risk ReductionFrom $500 consultation

Personalized screening and prevention built around genetics, family history, lifestyle, metabolism, vaccines, exposures, age, and long-term risk.

Prevent what we can. Detect what we cannot. Personalize the rest.

Recommendations may include conventional screening, advanced imaging, genetic assessment, and emerging blood-based testing when clinically appropriate.

10Mitochondrial HealthContact for pricing

A structured approach to the cellular systems that support energy, cognition, immune function, athletic performance, and healthy aging.

  • Exercise programming designed to improve mitochondrial density and function
  • Nutrition, sleep, fasting, cold-exposure, and supplement guidance
  • Physician-guided pharmacotherapy review when appropriate
  • Optional biomarkers to monitor response over time
11Peptide ClinicContact for pricing

Physician-guided evaluation of peptide therapies based on health history, clinical needs, treatment goals, and available evidence.

  • Initial medical consultation and eligibility review
  • Relevant laboratory and medication assessment
  • Individualized treatment planning when appropriate
  • Ongoing monitoring, follow-up, and protocol adjustments
12Ketamine InfusionContact for pricing

Medically supervised ketamine infusion services for appropriate patients following a comprehensive clinical screening.

  • Pre-treatment consultation and safety evaluation
  • Individualized infusion planning
  • Continuous monitoring during treatment
  • Post-infusion observation and follow-up guidance
